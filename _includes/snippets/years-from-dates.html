{%- comment -%} Reduces a date range to bare years, returned in __return. "August 2026-Present" -> "2026-Present" "August 2020-July 2025" -> "2020-2025" "2026-Present" -> "2026-Present" (already bare, unchanged) /about lists positions by year while /cv and the PDF give the month, so the short form is derived rather than stored. Storing both would be two fields saying the same thing, which is the duplication this data is meant to remove. Splits on the hyphen and keeps the last word of each side, so it does not care whether a month is present. A range with no hyphen comes back unchanged. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _parts = include.dates | split: '-' -%} {%- assign _years = '' -%} {%- for _part in _parts -%} {%- assign _word = _part | strip | split: ' ' | last -%} {%- if forloop.first -%} {%- assign _years = _word -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _years = _years | append: '-' | append: _word -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign __return = _years -%}
